Our services

Advisory built around the work in front of you.

Eight practices. One standard. We stay with an engagement from the first question through the filing, the closing or the guarantee.

01

Financial Advisory Services

Strategic financial advisory for corporates, financial institutions, and individuals — covering business structuring, financial planning, feasibility studies, and advisory on complex financial arrangements.

02

Financial Institutions Licensing

End-to-end support for obtaining regulatory licences from the Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) and other regulators, including Bureau De Change, Microfinance Bank, Primary Mortgage Bank, and other financial institution licences — covering application preparation, capital verification, and responses to regulatory deficiency queries.

03

Proof of Funds

Verification and documentation services to demonstrate the availability of funds for regulatory, transactional, or investment purposes, prepared to meet the requirements of regulators, banks, and counterparties.

Get Funds now

04

Sale of Properties and Advisory Services

Advisory and facilitation services for real estate transactions, including structuring of property-linked financial products, mortgage arrangements, and coordination between developers, financial institutions, and prospective buyers.

05

Tax Management

Tax planning, compliance, and advisory services designed to optimise tax positions and ensure compliance with applicable tax laws and regulations.

06

Management Consulting Services

Advisory support on organisational structuring, operations transformation, process design, and strategic business development for financial institutions and other enterprises.

07

Due Diligence and KYC Review

Comprehensive due diligence and Know-Your-Customer (KYC) reviews to support regulatory compliance, risk assessment, and informed decision-making in financial transactions and partnerships.

08

Business Development

Financial linkages for active businesses via Advance Payment Guarantees and Bank Guarantees from reputable financiers.
